# dblp mirror — data notice

This WebHarbor site is a mirror of the dblp computer science bibliography
(https://dblp.org/), operated by Schloss Dagstuhl — Leibniz Center for
Informatics. All bibliographic metadata served by this mirror was captured
from the public dblp.org website and its public XML/API endpoints on
2026-09-30; dblp releases all metadata as open data under the CC0 1.0
license (https://dblp.org/faq/1474392). The dblp name, logo and site design
are used here solely to reproduce the look and behavior of the upstream
service inside a deterministic offline benchmark environment; this mirror
is not affiliated with or endorsed by Schloss Dagstuhl.

Images shipped in static/images/ are fetched from their exact upstream
URLs and pinned per-file by SHA-256 in asset_inventory.json. See
provenance.json for the full capture declaration.
