Sell your tickets

Can't make it? List your verified tickets for resale and get paid when they sell. It's free to list, and you keep control of your price.

1. List

Sign in, open the order in My Account, and select the tickets you want to sell. Set your asking price per ticket.

2. Sell

Your listing appears to buyers browsing the event. You can edit your price or remove the listing anytime before the tickets sell.

3. Get paid

When your tickets sell, we transfer the tickets to the buyer and deposit your payout to your bank account.
