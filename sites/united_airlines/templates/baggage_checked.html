Size and weight

The maximum size of a checked bag is 30 in x 20 in x 12 in (76 cm x 52 cm x 30 cm) or 62 total linear inches, including handles and wheels.

Weight limits depend on your cabin and MileagePlus status:

Bags over 100 lb are not accepted. The overweight and oversized fees are in addition to any checked bag fee.

We can't accept bags that measure (length + width + height) more than 115 inches (292 centimeters), or bags weighing more than 100 pounds (45 kilograms). Musical instruments and assistive devices are exceptions: we accept musical instruments up to 165 pounds (75 kilograms), and all assistive devices travel for free. Extra bags are only accepted if space is available, and if your bag is both oversized and overweight, each charge applies.

Bag check process

  • Check your bag on time — every airport has a cutoff for checking bags; you can't check a bag after it.
  • Keep your claim check — the tag receipt you get at the kiosk; keep it until you have all your bags.
  • Connecting flights — bags usually transfer automatically; re-checking is only needed for some international connections.
  • Bag drop shortcut — prepay online, then drop your bag in the shortcut area at the airport.
  • Pack liquids carefully — pack all liquids in a resealable bag. Liquids more than 3.4 oz (100 ml) should be packed in your checked bag.
