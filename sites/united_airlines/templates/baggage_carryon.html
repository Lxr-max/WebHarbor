Carry-on bags

You can bring one carry-on bag and one personal item for free on most domestic and international flights. But there are a few rules.

Basic Economy

Basic Economy includes a personal item on all flights. A full-size carry-on is also included when flying to Canada, South America, across the Atlantic, or on international flights across the Pacific. MileagePlus members with Premier status and primary United cardmembers always get the free carry-on.

Traveler pro tips

In addition to your carry-on bag and personal item you can bring for free: a jacket or coat, an umbrella, things to read, food or items bought at the airport, mobility devices (including wheelchairs, canes and crutches), a car seat, child safety harness or stroller, a diaper bag and breast pump, and a camera.

Special items

  • Smart bags with lithium batteries must have the battery removed before boarding.
  • TSA liquids rule: containers no larger than 3.4 oz (100 mL) inside a quart-size clear sealable bag.
  • Powders: TSA recommends checked bags; up to 12 oz (350 mL) is allowed in carry-on on domestic flights.
