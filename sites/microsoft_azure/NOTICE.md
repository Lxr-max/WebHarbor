# microsoft_azure — upstream notice

This directory contains a WebHarbor research mirror of
https://azure.microsoft.com/ (probed reachable 2026-09-30). All content
records in source_data/ derive from real captures of the live site, its
public pricing-calculator JSON feeds, and the customers.microsoft.com
story API; all images under static/images/ were downloaded from the
upstream CDNs (see asset_inventory.json for per-file source URLs and
hashes). See provenance.json for the full capture inventory.

The four benchmark user accounts in source_data/users.json are authored
benchmark infrastructure shared across WebHarbor sites (password
TestPass123!), not upstream content.

This mirror is for offline research use only and is not affiliated with
or endorsed by Microsoft.
