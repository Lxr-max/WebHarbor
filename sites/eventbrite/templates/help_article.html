Who handles a refund request?

The event organizer sets the refund policy and handles refund requests. Sign in, open Tickets, select the order and tickets, then choose Request a refund and submit your details. The organizer should respond within five business days. Contact the organizer if that option is unavailable.

Cancelled or postponed events

If the organizer refuses or does not respond, Eventbrite may help when the event was cancelled within the last 45 days, or postponed for more than 90 days without a new date. Eligible buyers have 45 days and until the event is rescheduled to contact Eventbrite. Only payments processed through Eventbrite Payment Processing can be refunded by Eventbrite.
