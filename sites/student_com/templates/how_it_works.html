How to book on Student.com

We know that booking student accommodation can be stressful so we're here to help every step of the way! Here's our quick guide to the ways you can secure your next home.

Our booking types

We have three ways to book on Student.com. Here's a handy guide to the key differences.

Enquire
Step1

Submit quick enquiries for the properties you're interested in.

Step2

The properties will contact you to discuss details and answer any questions.

Step3

Once you've decided which room is perfect for you, you can confirm your booking directly with the property.

Apply
Step1

Submit a full application for the property you're interested in.

Step2

The property will confirm your booking or contact you to discuss further.

Step3

You'll receive payment instructions to confirm your booking, either paying through Student.com or directly to the property.

Instant Book
Step1

Submit an online booking form for your chosen property, including preferred payment plan and personal details.

Step2

Sign your contract online.

Step3

Your booking is confirmed! Get packed and ready to start on your next chapter of student life!

Connect with our team

If you have any questions please don't hesitate to contact us by WhatsApp, live chat, phone or email - we're always happy to help!
