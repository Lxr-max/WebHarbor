Our Privacy Policy

Learn how we collect, use, and protect your personal information at Student.com.

1. Important information and who we are

Student.com, and/or its affiliates and related companies ("Student.com", "we", or "us") operate the Student.com website and mobile applications. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing our Site and using our services, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

We respect your right to privacy and will only process personal information you provide to us in accordance with this policy.

Controller

Our Site is operated by Student.com Ventures Limited, acting as a data controller.

Contact details

If you have any questions about how we collect, store and use personal information, you can email us at contact@student.com.

Changes to this privacy policy

We may from time to time make changes to this Privacy Policy. It is important that the personal data we hold about you is accurate and current — keep us informed if your personal data changes.

2. The data we collect about you

Personal data, or personal information, means any information about an individual from which that person can be identified. We may collect, use, store and transfer different kinds of personal data about you, which we have grouped together as follows:

  • Identity Data includes first name, last name and similar identifiers.
  • Contact Data includes email address and telephone numbers.
  • Usage Data includes information about how you use our website, such as the properties you view and save.
  • Marketing Data includes your preferences for receiving marketing from us.

3. How we use your data

  • To operate your account and show your saved properties and inquiries.
  • To pass your inquiry to the property you contacted — the property then contacts you directly.
  • To keep the site safe and to prevent fraud (see our Avoid Scams and Fraud guide).
  • To send you marketing about other properties if you asked us to when sending an inquiry.

4. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to processing or request data portability. Contact contact@student.com to exercise these rights.

5. Third-party links

Our Site may contain links to third party sites, including property websites and job boards. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites — a property's website will have its own privacy policy.

6. Data security and retention

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to keep your data safe. We keep your inquiry history for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the service.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email contact@student.com or use our contact form.
