Summary of our Terms

Student.com is the world's leading marketplace for student housing, helping students find their perfect home during their higher education studies. The platform connects students with accommodation providers; we do not own, operate or manage the properties listed.

  • The marketplace: listings, availability and prices are provided by properties and their teams. "From" prices reflect the cheapest room type currently available and can change.
  • Inquiries: sending an inquiry through Student.com is free and non-binding. Any agreement (lease, deposit, payment plan) is made directly between you and the property.
  • Accounts: keep your login details safe. You are responsible for activity on your account, including the inquiries and messages you send.
  • Acceptable use: do not scrape, resell or misrepresent listings; do not use the platform for fraudulent purposes. We remove listings and accounts that break these rules.
  • Content: property photos, summaries and review data are supplied by properties and their data partners. Trademarks belong to their respective owners.
