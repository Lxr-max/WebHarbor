Bell Calendar

One of the most familiar images of the NYSE on the evening news is the loud ringing of a bell, signaling the opening or closing of the day’s trading. Trading floor bells are more than just a colorful tradition — they are critical to the orderly functioning of the marketplace, assuring that no trades take place before the opening or after the close.
