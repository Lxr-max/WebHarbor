Print shipping labels from your home or office

Click-N-Ship lets you pay for postage online and print labels with a few steps:

  1. Sign in to your USPS.com account.
  2. Enter sender and recipient addresses.
  3. Enter package weight and dimensions.
  4. Choose a service and any extra services (insurance, signature, Certified Mail).
