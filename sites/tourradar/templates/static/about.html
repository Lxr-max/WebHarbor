About TourRadar

TourRadar is the world's most trusted platform to explore and book multi-day organized adventures. From safaris and river cruises to trekking expeditions and cultural journeys, we connect travelers with 2,500 vetted tour operators across 6 continents.

Founded with a simple belief — “Customer comes first” — TourRadar streamlines every step: comparing real traveler reviews, securing flexible payment options, and providing 24/7 customer support before, during and after your trip.

  • 500k experiences shared by travelers
  • 4.6 stars from 10,000+ Trustpilot reviews
  • 24/7 customer support from real adventure specialists
