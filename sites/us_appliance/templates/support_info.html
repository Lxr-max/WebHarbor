    About Us


    Thanks for visiting US Appliance, the best way to shop for and purchase home appliances and electronics. We offer all of the leading appliance brands and our appliance experts are on hand to answer your questions, eliminating a lot of the guesswork in making your choice.


    Founded in 1963, we're among the largest appliance and electronics retailers nationally, and we are the largest high-end appliance retailer in Michigan. Online since 1999, we've helped hundreds of thousands of customers all across the country with their home appliance needs. Speak to very experienced, factory-trained, non-commissioned sales consultants ready to assist you in selecting what you need, not what pushy salesmen want to sell. Orders are quickly routed for fast delivery.


    US Appliance has been celebrated in PC World Magazine's "Best of the Web," and has also been recognized in articles from publications such as The New York Times, Woman's Day Magazine, Internet Retailer and others.
