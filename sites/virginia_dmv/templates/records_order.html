Request Your DMV Record

You can view and order your own driver record or the record for any vehicle you own. Records you view online may be viewed again within 5 days of the original purchase at no charge. A driving or vehicle record costs $8.00 online ($9.00 by mail); a certified copy adds $5.00.

Which record?

To request a vehicle record you need the title number and vehicle identification number (VIN) — both are shown for your vehicles.
