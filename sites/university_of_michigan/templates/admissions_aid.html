Types of Aid

Students at U-M are typically awarded three types of financial aid: scholarships and grants, loans, and Work-Study.

If You're an International Student

International undergraduate students who hold temporary visas such as the student visa are not eligible for U.S. federal financial aid and have very limited opportunities for University of Michigan scholarships. International students on temporary visas are not eligible for financial aid, and are expected to pay the full cost of attendance.
