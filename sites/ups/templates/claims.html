Getting Started

Start a claim within 60 days of the scheduled delivery for packages that have been lost or damaged, or if a collect on delivery (C.O.D.) payment wasn’t received.

2

Under Review

Check your email — while we review your claim, we may send time-sensitive notifications of action needed, like additional information or photos. Keep any damaged contents and packaging.

3

Resolution

Unless additional investigation is required, you can typically expect a resolution to your claim in 8 to 10 business days. Once approved, payment is sent to the shipper via electronic funds transfer (EFT).

What To Keep In Mind

  • You’ll need your tracking number to get started.
  • Keep any invoice that can provide the value of the package.
  • If you have a damaged package, hold on to the contents and the packaging.
  • If you are the receiver and want updates, reach out to whoever shipped the package with us.

FAQ

What does my current claim status mean?

  • Claim Issued: the claim was issued and paperwork was sent to the shipper of record.
  • Shipment Inspection Scheduled: UPS has scheduled an inspection of the damaged package.
  • Claim Reported – Supporting Document(s) Received: your submitted documents or photos have been successfully uploaded to your claim.
  • Claim Review in Progress: your claim is being reviewed by our claims department.

Why can’t I submit my claim on UPS.com?

  • Ground Saver Packages: claims are accepted until the exception scan that indicates the package has been handed over to the Post Office is recorded.
  • Past Liability: submitting a claim has to be taken care of within a timely manner of delivery — within 60 days of the scheduled delivery.
